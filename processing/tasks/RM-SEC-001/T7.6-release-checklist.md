# T7.6 — Release checklist

**Roadmap:** RM-SEC-001 / G7 / T7.6

## Deliverable

`docs/release-checklist.md` lists the evidence, decisions and boundaries
required before MI-P6. It blocks handoff until:

- clean install, discovery, build and artifact allowlist checks pass;
- production audit is zero and full-tree highs are resolved or excepted;
- G2–G6 evidence, continuous gates and CodeQL pass;
- every advisory is resolved or has an unexpired owner-approved exception;
- no production high or critical exception exists;
- a named human security owner signs and records the MI-P6 decision.

The checklist also preserves the browser-only boundary and the draft-PR
restrictions.
